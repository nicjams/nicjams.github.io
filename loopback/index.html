    Input & timing

    Input on the computer

    A mixer that sends each channel over USB shows them all here — pick the one your instrument is on. If it only sends its main mix, send the computer's return to your headphones (not the main mix), or the loop gets recorded into your solo.

    Latency compensation ms

    How long a sound takes to go out the speakers and back in. Measure plays eight clicks: on speakers, just let the input hear them; on headphones, mute your strings and strum along with each one.
