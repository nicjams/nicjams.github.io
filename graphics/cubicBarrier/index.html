Cubic Barrier

Penetration-free rods, braids & knots

Without the CCD filter only the barrier's force stands between two strands. Yank a strand quickly through its neighbour with the mouse: it tunnels, and the intersection counter lights up. With the filter on it never happens.

How the solver works
cubic log quadratic live gaps
  1. Cubic barrier. Each pair closer than ĝ gets energy κ·ψ(g) with ψ(g) = −(2/3ĝ)(g−ĝ)³. It is finite at contact.
  2. Dynamic stiffness. κ = m/g² + nᵀHn is recomputed at every Newton step from the mass and the elastic Hessian, so the force still diverges as g → 0.
  3. CCD-filtered step. Each Newton step is cut to the fraction that CCD over 1.25× the step allows. Once the accepted fractions sum to ¼, Δt shrinks to match. No energy line search, and no step ever crosses.
  4. Strain limiting. The same barrier on each edge's stretch keeps it under τ.
