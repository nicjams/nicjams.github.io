Each band meets a tile edge at its midpoint, square to the edge, so any two tiles fit no matter how they are turned. Three kinds of path do the joining: an arc of radius ½ around a corner, an arc of radius ³⁄₂ skipping one edge, and a straight line to the opposite edge. The grid is wrapped into a torus, so every component is a closed loop: a link, recolored whenever a turn splits or merges its components.
