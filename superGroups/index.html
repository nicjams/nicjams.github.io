ABOUT THESE MUSICIANS

These are original, synthetic musical identities: pocket, restless, and spacious players on bass, keys, lead, and drums. Their note choices come from a small trained model; the sound comes from browser synthesizers.

Explore saved model performances here. This is an early experiment in musical interaction, with sparse passages and imperfect replies—not artist imitations or live model inference.
